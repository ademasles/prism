# Template — Executive One-Pager

For rapid executive understanding when there's no single binary decision on the table. Must land the point in a 60-second skim. One page, hard limit.

## Instructions
- Headline states the takeaway, not the topic. ("Cloud migration is on track for Q3; one risk needs a call" — not "Cloud Migration Update.")
- Lead with the "so what," then support. Numbers over adjectives.
- End with what happens next / what (if anything) you need.

## Structure

```
# <Headline takeaway — the conclusion, not the topic>

**Bottom line:** <2–3 sentences a busy exec could repeat accurately after one read>

**Situation:** <3–4 lines: where things stand>

**What it means for you / the business:** <the implication — why they should care>

**Key numbers:**
- <metric> — <value / trend>
- <metric> — <value / trend>

**Risks / watch items:** <the 1–2 things that could change the picture>

**What happens next:** <next steps, owners, and anything you need from the reader>
```
