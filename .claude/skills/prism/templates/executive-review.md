# Template — Executive Review

Review the draft as a demanding senior consulting partner. Direct and useful over polite. The point is to catch what would embarrass you in front of leadership *before* it reaches them. Full definitions and anchors: `references/quality-gates.md`.

## Instructions
- Run all 14 gates; call out each failure with a specific fix.
- Score the 10 dimensions 1–5, average, apply the verdict rule.
- Name the single most important fix. Don't hedge.

## Verdict rules
- `< 2.5` → weak · `2.5–<3.5` → usable · `3.5–<4.5` → strong · `≥4.5` → executive_ready
