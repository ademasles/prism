# Template — Presentation Outline

For a message delivered live, where the value is narrative, pacing, and real-time facilitation. Maps to speaker moments, not slides. If the audience will read asynchronously, write a memo instead.

## Instructions
- Design the arc, not the slides. Open with stakes, not agenda.
- Every beat advances the argument toward the ask.
- Plan the Q&A — the objections will come live; decide your answers now.

## Structure

```
# Presentation Outline — <title> (<audience>, <time>)

## Outcome
<what the audience should decide/believe/do by the end>

## Opening — the hook & stakes
<the tension or question that makes them lean in — 30–60 seconds of talk>

## Act 1 — <the situation / why this matters>
<key beats; the point of this act>

## Act 2 — <the argument / the shift>
<key beats; evidence moments; the turn>

## Act 3 — <the resolution / what we do>
<key beats leading to the ask>

## The ask
<the specific thing you want from the room, stated plainly>

## Anticipated Q&A
| Likely question / objection | Your answer |
|---|---|
| … | … |

## Close
<the one line you want them to remember and repeat>
```
