# Template — Clarification Questions

Ask **5–7** questions. Each must be one whose answer would change the recommendation. Draw from the bank below; adapt wording to the request. If the user doesn't answer, every gap becomes a labelled Working Assumption.

## Instructions
- Lead with the two questions that most change the artifact choice (audience + required outcome).
- Prefer specific over generic ("Who signs off the budget?" beats "Who is the audience?").
- Do not ask more than 7. Do not ask for information you can reasonably infer and label.

## Question bank (pick 5–7)

1. **Audience & decider** — Who exactly receives this, and who actually makes the call? (roles, seniority, how many)
2. **Current state** — What does this audience believe, misunderstand, fear, or ignore about this today?
3. **Desired outcome** — After this lands, what should they *decide, do, or believe* that they wouldn't otherwise? (the single most important question)
4. **Decision or awareness?** — Is a specific decision/approval needed, or is this awareness/alignment only?
5. **Objections** — What are the strongest reasons this audience would say no, delay, or push back?
6. **Trusted evidence** — What kind of proof does this audience find credible? (numbers, references, demos, precedent)
7. **Format & forum** — Will this be read or presented live? What forum, how much time, any format expected/required?
8. **Stakes & timing** — What happens if nothing changes? Is there a deadline or "why now"?
9. **Constraints** — Anything fixed (budget, prior commitments, sensitivities, no-go options) I should respect?

## Output format

```
## Clarification Questions
1. <question>
2. <question>
... (5–7 total)

*If you can't answer some, I'll proceed with clearly labelled assumptions.*
```
