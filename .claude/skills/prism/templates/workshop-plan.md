# Template — Workshop Plan

For co-creation, alignment, conflict resolution, or genuine buy-in that broadcast can't achieve — people must participate to own the outcome. If you already have the answer and just need approval, this is the wrong artifact (use a decision memo).

## Instructions
- Define the concrete outputs the session must produce — a workshop without decisions/artifacts is just a meeting.
- Assign decision rights: who converges, who decides, what's in scope.
- Design activities that force participation, not presentation.

## Structure

```
# Workshop Plan — <topic>

## Objective & desired end state
<what must be true when people leave the room — decisions made, alignment reached, artifact produced>

## Participants & roles
| Person / role | Why they're here | Decision rights |
|---|---|---|
| … | … | <decides / advises / informs> |

## Pre-reads
<what participants must read/prepare beforehand so the session isn't spent on context>

## Agenda
| Time | Activity | Format | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Frame & objective | plenary | shared goal |
| … | … | <breakout / vote / dot-map / …> | <decision / list / map> |

## Decisions / outputs to be produced
<the tangible results the session commits to>

## Facilitation notes
<how to handle dominant voices, conflict, drift; where consensus vs decision-by-owner applies>

## Follow-ups
<who owns what after; how outputs get ratified and communicated>
```
