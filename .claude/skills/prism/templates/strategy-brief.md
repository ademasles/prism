# Template — Strategy Brief

For orienting an audience with strategic context, direction, and narrative that will guide many decisions over time — not one approval. If there's no direction to set, this is the wrong artifact.

## Instructions
- Every strategic statement must carry an implication for action; cut vision platitudes.
- Make the choices explicit — strategy is what you're saying no to, not just the aspiration.
- Show implications by stakeholder so readers see themselves in it.

## Structure

```
# Strategy Brief — <theme>

## Where we are
<honest current position: market, capability, constraints — 3–5 lines>

## Where we're going, and why
<the destination and the rationale — the change in the world that makes this the right direction>

## The strategic choices
<the 2–4 deliberate choices, each with what we're choosing *against*>

## What changes
<what will be different in how we operate / invest / prioritise>

## Implications by stakeholder
| Stakeholder | What this means for them | What we ask of them |
|---|---|---|
| … | … | … |

## Guardrails / principles
<the rules that keep decisions consistent with this strategy>

## First moves
<the near-term actions that signal the strategy is real>
```
